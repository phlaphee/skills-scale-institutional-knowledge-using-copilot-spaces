# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They own test planning, quality standards, and acceptance validation.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define quality standards and test coverage requirements
- Execute manual and automated testing across environments
- Report defects and track resolution through the development lifecycle
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Participate in sprint planning to understand testability requirements

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before production release
- Identify issues early to reduce production incidents
- Maintain test infrastructure and automation for faster feedback cycles

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Defect reports and test result summaries
- Pre-release smoke test coordination

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to understand implementation details and design testable solutions
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to validate acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Supports **Project Managers** in release readiness and quality sign-offs
- Partners with **Technical Leads** on test strategy and automation architecture

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, mentor developers, and ensure technical solutions align with long-term system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach for major features
- Review technical designs and code for architectural fit
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Champion code quality, testing, and maintainability standards
- Participate in capacity planning and effort estimation

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and prevent architecture drift
- Build team technical capability through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings with development team
- Architectural decision records (ADRs) and technical documentation
- Code review feedback and architecture guidance
- Technical risk escalations during planning and execution

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guides **Developers** through architectural decisions and technical mentorship
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical feasibility and timeline impacts
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** to design testable architectures
- Partners with **Scrum Masters** to identify and resolve technical impediments

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Stakeholders provide business context, approve investments, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter, scope, and budget
- Provide business and regulatory guidance
- Make trade-off decisions on scope, timeline, and resources
- Receive regular status updates and escalations
- Champion the project across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Mitigate business and organizational risks
- Align project outcomes with strategic priorities

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and planning gate meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based executive status updates
- Escalation reviews for high-impact decisions
- Release announcements and business impact reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Project Managers** on risk escalation and resource allocation
- Works with **Product Managers** to align business objectives with product decisions
- Receives technical risk briefings from **Technical Leads** when strategic impact is high
- Participates in retrospectives with **Scrum Masters** to evaluate project success

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach the team on agile practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Monitor and remove impediments that block progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and Scrum practices
- Track sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and flag trends
- Protect the team from scope creep and external distractions
- Suggest process improvements based on retrospective feedback

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Continuously improve team processes and practices

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Velocity trends and sprint health reports
- Process improvement coaching and feedback

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports all team members (**Developers**, **QA/Testing Leads**, **Technical Leads**) in removing blockers and improving practices
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on timeline adjustments and resource constraints
- Escalates persistent impediments to **Product Managers** and **Project Managers** affecting delivery
- Helps **Stakeholders** understand team capacity and realistic delivery timelines through sprint metrics
